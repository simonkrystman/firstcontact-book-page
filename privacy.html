Privacy on this website

This page describes the supplied First Contact website. Last updated 24 November 2026.

Reading this site

This website does not include analytics, advertising trackers, newsletter forms or embedded third-party media, and it sets no cookies of its own. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and maintain the site.

Email address protection

This site is delivered through Cloudflare. To protect the contact email address from automated harvesting, Cloudflare replaces it in the page with an encoded version and adds a small script, served from this site’s own address, that restores it in your browser. If JavaScript is turned off, the address appears as “[email protected]”; you can reach Simon through LinkedIn instead. Cloudflare may also process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site. No cookies were observed on this site when this notice was written.

Purchases

Purchase links take you to IngramSpark (paperback) or Amazon UK (Kindle ebook). Checkout, payment, delivery and related personal information are handled there under their own policies. This site does not collect payment details.

Email and external links

If you choose to email Simon, your email service sends the information you include. Simon can be contacted at skrystman@12ronnies.com. LinkedIn, YouTube and other external services apply their own policies when you visit them.

Future changes

This notice should be updated if a mailing-list form, analytics, embedded media or other services are added.
