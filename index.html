About the book

What if First Contact has already begun?

We imagined an encounter with intelligence arriving from the stars. What if it has begun within our own civilisation?

In First Contact: We Built the Aliens, Simon Krystman explores AI through the lens of our encounter with engineered, non-biological cognition. Its capabilities can be impressive while its processes and constraints differ from ours. Creation does not guarantee control, and fluent language does not settle the question of consciousness.

From work and learning to creativity, relationships and human worth, this concise book asks what changes when significant cognitive capability is no longer confined to biological minds.

What should we delegate? How should we judge the answers? And where does responsibility remain?

For readers who want to think more clearly about living with AI, this is an invitation to look again.

Questions

Is this science fiction?

No. It is accessible nonfiction. The First Contact idea is a lens for examining AI, not a story about visitors from space.

Does the book assume machines are conscious?

No. It uses the First Contact frame without assuming that AI is conscious, alive or human-like in its desires, and it asks what follows when capabilities and constraints differ from ours.

Do I need to read the longer book first?

No. This concise edition stands on its own.

How was AI used to write it?

The book discloses its collaboration with AI. Simon Krystman retained editorial responsibility for what was kept, challenged and rewritten.
